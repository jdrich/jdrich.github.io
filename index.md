Technical change is happening faster than ever. New paradigms and conventions for defining software systems are being developed daily. The justification for technology - what makes it worth doing, what blocks it, what it costs and what it's worth - are changing constantly. The underlying needs aren't.

## Repositories

A few projects.

- [sttttts](https://github.com/jdrich/sttttts) Speech in, speech out, all on CPU. Moonshine + Kokoro, nothing leaves the box.
- [catmouse](https://github.com/jdrich/catmouse) Live prompt-injection eval. Cat attacks, mouse defends, eight levels, no tools actually fire.
- [pipio](https://github.com/jdrich/pipio) Evented PHP for service-oriented apps — logging and AMQP included.
- [lines](https://github.com/jdrich/lines) A shared canvas. Lines, curves, other people drawing at the same time.
- [math](https://github.com/jdrich/math) Timed arithmetic. Pick an operation, beat your record.
- [vixen_js](https://github.com/jdrich/vixen_js) JSONP poll and signal. Small on purpose.
